Privacy Policy

How Utivaro handles data, files and privacy.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Overview

Utivaro is designed to provide useful online tools while minimizing unnecessary collection of personal information.

Whenever technically possible, tools process information directly inside your browser rather than sending it to a remote server.

Browser-based processing

Some Utivaro tools perform their main processing locally on your device.

Examples may include image conversion, text analysis, calculations and unit conversions.

When a tool runs locally in your browser, the data you enter or files you select do not need to be uploaded to Utivaro for the tool to perform its main function.

Files and user input

Utivaro does not intentionally store files processed entirely inside your browser.

If future tools require server-side processing, the relevant tool page or this policy may be updated to explain how those files are handled.

Information automatically collected

Like most websites, Utivaro may receive basic technical information when you access the site.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate location based on IP address, referring page and general usage information.

Analytics

Utivaro may use analytics services in the future to understand how visitors use the website and which tools are most useful.

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Utivaro may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising services to support the cost of operating and improving the website.

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Utivaro or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to Utivaro and/or other websites on the Internet.

Where applicable, users may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner sites privacy information.

Cookies

Utivaro itself aims to keep cookies to a minimum.

Third-party services, including analytics and advertising providers such as Google, may use cookies or similar technologies when those services are enabled.

Depending on your location and applicable law, consent or privacy controls may be presented before certain advertising or analytics technologies are used.

Third-party services

Utivaro may rely on third-party infrastructure or services for hosting, security, analytics, advertising or other website functionality.

These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

External links

Utivaro may contain links to external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's privacy

Utivaro is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Utivaro grows and new tools or services are introduced.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Utivaro's privacy practices, please use the contact information provided on Utivaro's Contact page.
