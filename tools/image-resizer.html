Resize JPG, PNG and WebP images to exact pixel dimensions, preserve their proportions and download the result—all in your browser.

Resize an image to exact dimensions

Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file, enter the width or height you need, then select Resize Image. With Keep aspect ratio enabled, the other dimension updates automatically so the image is not stretched. Turn it off only when you need a specific width and height.

Choose the right image format

Keep the source format or export as JPG, PNG or WebP. JPG suits photographs and broad compatibility, PNG is useful when you need transparency, and WebP often provides efficient web delivery. To convert without resizing, use the dedicated JPG to WebP converter or WebP to JPG converter.

Control quality and file size

The quality slider applies to JPG and WebP output. A lower setting can reduce the download size, while a higher setting retains more detail. The result panel lets you compare the original and resized file sizes before downloading.

Private local processing

Your image is decoded, resized and exported locally with your browser's Canvas API; it is not uploaded to Utivaro. If your source is an Apple photo, first use the HEIC to JPG converter, then resize the JPG here.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I resize?

The resizer supports JPG, PNG and WebP files in modern browsers.

Can I keep the aspect ratio?

Yes. Keep aspect ratio is enabled by default so changing one dimension updates the other.

Can I change the output format?

Yes. You can keep the original format or export to JPG, PNG or WebP.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing and export happen locally in your browser.
