Resize images online

Resize JPG, PNG and WebP images by entering exact pixel dimensions. Keep the original aspect ratio or set a custom width and height.

Choose an output format

Keep the original format or export the resized image as JPG, PNG or WebP. For JPG and WebP you can adjust the quality setting.

Private local processing

The image is decoded, resized and exported in your browser using the Canvas API. It does not need to be uploaded to Utivaro.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I resize?

The resizer supports JPG, PNG and WebP files in modern browsers.

Can I keep the aspect ratio?

Yes. Keep aspect ratio is enabled by default so changing one dimension updates the other.

Can I change the output format?

Yes. You can keep the original format or export to JPG, PNG or WebP.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing and export happen locally in your browser.
