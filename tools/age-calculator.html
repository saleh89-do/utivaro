How to use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth, then choose the date you want to calculate your age on. By default, the calculator uses today's date.

Select Calculate Age to see your exact age in years, months and days, along with additional statistics such as total months, weeks and days.

How age is calculated

Calendar rule: month anniversaries use the last available day when a month is shorter. February 29 anniversaries fall on February 28 in non-leap years. Total months and weeks are approximate.

Your exact age is calculated by comparing the calendar year, month and day of your birth date with the selected calculation date.

This approach accounts for different month lengths and leap years instead of simply dividing the total number of days by 365.

Age calculator example

If someone was born on January 1, 2000 and calculates their age on January 1, 2026, the result is exactly 26 years.

Common uses for an age calculator

An age calculator can be useful for checking eligibility requirements, completing forms, planning birthdays, calculating age on a specific historical date, or quickly finding how much time has passed since a birth date.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate your age on. The calculator will show your age in years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. You can select any valid date after your date of birth as the calculation date.

Does the calculator show total days lived?

Yes. It also displays approximate total months, total weeks and total days between the dates.

Is the Utivaro Age Calculator free?

Yes. The tool is free and does not require an account.
