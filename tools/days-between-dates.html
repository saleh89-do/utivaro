How many days are between two dates?

Enter a start date and an end date above. Utivaro will instantly calculate the number of calendar days between the two dates.

How does the date calculator work?

The calculator converts both selected dates into standardized calendar values and measures the difference between them.

The result is then displayed in days, weeks, approximate months and hours.

Example

If the start date is January 1 and the end date is January 31, the difference is 30 days.

Does the calculator include the start date?

No. This calculator measures the elapsed time between the two dates. For example, January 1 to January 2 equals one day.

Is this date calculator free?

Yes. Utivaro's Days Between Dates Calculator is completely free and works directly in your browser without requiring an account.
