Calculate Days Between Two Dates

Utivaro's Days Between Dates Calculator makes it easy to find the elapsed time between two calendar dates.

Select a start date and an end date above and the calculator will show the difference in days, weeks, approximate months and hours.

How to Use the Date Difference Calculator

  1. Select your start date.
  2. Select your end date.
  3. The calculator will automatically calculate the difference when both dates are selected.
  4. You can also select Calculate to calculate the result again.

How Is the Number of Days Calculated?

The calculator measures the elapsed calendar time between the selected dates.

For example, the difference between January 1 and January 2 is one day. The difference between January 1 and January 31 is 30 days.

Does the Calculator Include the Start Date?

No. The result represents the elapsed time between the two dates.

This means two consecutive dates, such as March 10 and March 11, have a difference of one day.

Days to Weeks

One week contains seven days. The calculator divides the total number of days by seven to display the difference in weeks.

For example, 14 days equals 2 weeks, while 30 days is approximately 4.29 weeks.

Days to Months

Calendar months have different lengths, so converting a date difference into months is not as simple as dividing by one fixed number of days.

Utivaro therefore displays an approximate month value based on the average length of a month.

Days to Hours

Each full day contains 24 hours. The calculator multiplies the number of elapsed days by 24 to show the equivalent number of hours.

For example, 30 days equals 720 hours.

What If the End Date Is Before the Start Date?

You can enter the dates in either order. The calculator displays the absolute difference between them.

If the end date occurs before the start date, the result will also indicate that the dates were entered in reverse chronological order.

Common Uses for a Date Difference Calculator

Calculating days between dates can be useful for project planning, deadlines, travel, events, contracts, schedules, countdowns and general date calculations.

It can also help determine how much time has passed between two historical dates or how many days remain between two future dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are between January 1 and January 31?

There are 30 elapsed days between January 1 and January 31.

Does this calculator count leap years?

Yes. Because the calculation uses the actual selected calendar dates, leap-year dates such as February 29 are accounted for.

Can I calculate dates from different years?

Yes. The start and end dates can be in different months or different years.

Can the end date be earlier than the start date?

Yes. The calculator will still show the number of days separating the dates and indicate that the end date is earlier.

Are the months exact?

No. The month result is approximate because calendar months contain different numbers of days.

Is the Days Between Dates Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use and does not require registration.
