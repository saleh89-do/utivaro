Calculate a restaurant tip instantly

Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage and set the number of people. The calculator shows the tip, total bill and per-person split.

How to calculate a tip

Tip amount = bill × tip percentage ÷ 100. The total bill is the original bill plus the tip. When splitting a bill, the total is divided by the number of people.

Quick tip percentages

Use the preset buttons for 10%, 15%, 18%, 20% or 25%, or enter any custom percentage you prefer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% tip?

Multiply the bill by 0.20. For example, a 20% tip on $50 is $10.

Can I split the bill between multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of people to see both the tip per person and total per person.

Can I use a custom tip percentage?

Yes. Enter any non-negative percentage in the tip field.

Does the calculator add taxes automatically?

No. Enter the bill amount you want the tip calculation to use.
