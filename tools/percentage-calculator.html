How to calculate a percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

For example, 25% of 200 is 50.

How to calculate what percentage one number is of another

Divide the first number by the second number, then multiply the result by 100.

How to calculate percentage increase

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100.
