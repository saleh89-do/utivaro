Free Online Unit Converter

Utivaro's Unit Converter helps you convert common measurement units quickly and accurately from your browser.

Choose a category, enter a value, select the starting unit and destination unit, and the converted result appears instantly.

Supported Unit Conversion Categories

The converter currently supports seven common measurement categories: length, weight, temperature, digital storage, area, volume and speed.

Length Converter

Convert between meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, miles, yards, feet and inches.

Common examples include meters to feet, kilometers to miles and inches to centimeters.

Weight Converter

Convert kilograms, grams, milligrams, pounds, ounces and metric tons.

For example, you can quickly convert kilograms to pounds or pounds to kilograms.

Temperature Converter

Convert temperatures between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

This is useful for weather, cooking, science and international temperature comparisons.

Digital Storage Converter

Convert between bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes.

The digital storage conversions in this tool use a 1024-based relationship between units.

Area Converter

Convert square meters, square kilometers, square feet, square yards, acres and hectares.

Area conversion can be useful for property, land measurement, construction and planning.

Volume Converter

Convert liters, milliliters, cubic meters, US gallons, US quarts and US cups.

Speed Converter

Convert meters per second, kilometers per hour, miles per hour and knots.

For example, you can convert kilometers per hour to miles per hour when comparing vehicle speeds.

How to Convert Units

  1. Select the measurement category.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Choose the starting unit under From.
  4. Choose the destination unit under To.
  5. View the converted result instantly.

You can also use the Swap Units button to reverse the conversion direction.

Common Unit Conversion Examples

Some frequently used conversions include: meters to feet, kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, pounds to kilograms, Celsius to Fahrenheit, liters to gallons, acres to hectares and kilometers per hour to miles per hour.

Fast Browser-Based Calculations

All unit calculations are performed directly in your browser.

You do not need to create an account, upload information or install software to use the converter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many meters are in one kilometer?

One kilometer equals 1,000 meters.

How many pounds are in one kilogram?

One kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Select Temperature as the category, choose Celsius under From and Fahrenheit under To, then enter the temperature.

Can I convert kilometers per hour to miles per hour?

Yes. Choose Speed and select kilometers per hour and miles per hour as the two units.

Can I convert MB to GB?

Yes. Choose Digital Storage, select Megabytes as the starting unit and Gigabytes as the destination unit.

Is the Unit Converter free?

Yes. The Utivaro Unit Converter is free and requires no signup.
