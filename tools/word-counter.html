Free Online Word Counter

Utivaro's Word Counter lets you quickly check the number of words and characters in any piece of text.

Paste or type your content into the box above and the statistics update automatically. No signup or software installation is required.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the word and character counts instantly.
  3. Check additional statistics such as sentences, paragraphs and reading time.
  4. Use the Copy Text button if you want to copy the text back to your clipboard.

What Does the Word Counter Measure?

The tool provides several useful text statistics at the same time.

These include total words, total characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

Word Count

Word count measures the number of words contained in your text. It is useful when writing content that needs to meet a specific length requirement.

Common examples include essays, articles, reports, assignments, product descriptions, applications and social media content.

Character Count

Character count measures the total number of characters in your text, including spaces.

The tool also shows a separate count for characters without spaces. This can be useful for platforms, forms or assignments that have character limits.

Sentence and Paragraph Counter

Utivaro also estimates the number of sentences and paragraphs in your text.

These statistics can help when reviewing the structure and length of articles, essays and other written content.

Estimated Reading Time

The reading-time estimate gives you an approximate idea of how long it may take an average reader to read your text.

Reading speed varies from person to person, so the result should be treated as an estimate rather than an exact measurement.

Is My Text Private?

Yes. The counting is performed directly inside your browser.

Your text does not need to be uploaded to a Utivaro server for the Word Counter to perform its main function.

Who Can Use a Word Counter?

A word counter can be useful for students, writers, editors, marketers, bloggers, teachers, researchers and anyone working with written content.

It can also help when preparing emails, advertisements, social posts, descriptions or documents with specific word or character limits.
