Free Online Word Counter

Utivaro Word Counter helps you instantly calculate the number of words and characters in any text.

It is useful for articles, essays, social media posts, assignments, product descriptions, emails and other writing tasks.

How to use the Word Counter

Simply type or paste your text into the box. The statistics update automatically while you type.

Is my text private?

Yes. The counting happens directly inside your browser. Your text does not need to be uploaded to a Utivaro server for this tool to work.

What does the tool count?

The tool currently calculates words, total characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.
