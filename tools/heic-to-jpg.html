Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC is commonly used by Apple devices because it can store high-quality photos efficiently. JPG is more broadly supported by websites, document tools and older software.

Private browser-based conversion

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC?

HEIC is an image container format commonly used by Apple devices for efficient photo storage.

How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

Choose a HEIC or HEIF file, adjust JPG quality, select Convert to JPG and download the result.

Is my HEIC photo uploaded to Utivaro?

No. The selected file is processed in your browser.

Why might a HEIC file fail to convert?

Some HEIC files use variations or metadata that browser conversion libraries may not support. Trying another file or a current browser can help.
