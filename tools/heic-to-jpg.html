How to convert HEIC or HEIF to JPG

Choose an HEIC or HEIF photo, set the JPG quality, select Convert to JPG, then preview and download the result. No account or software installation is required.

Why change an Apple photo to JPG?

HEIC is commonly used by iPhone, iPad and other Apple devices for efficient photo storage. JPG is easier to open in older software and is broadly accepted by websites, document editors and sharing services.

Choose a useful JPG quality

A higher quality setting retains more visual detail but usually creates a larger file. A lower setting may be useful for email or web uploads. After converting, use the online Image Resizer if you also need exact pixel dimensions.

Private browser-based conversion

Your selected photo is processed on your device rather than uploaded to Utivaro. The HEIC conversion library itself is downloaded from a public CDN. For other formats, try WebP to JPG or convert an existing JPEG with JPG to WebP.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC?

HEIC is an image container format commonly used by Apple devices for efficient photo storage.

How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

Choose a HEIC or HEIF file, adjust JPG quality, select Convert to JPG and download the result.

Is my HEIC photo uploaded to Utivaro?

No. The selected file is processed in your browser.

Why might a HEIC file fail to convert?

Some HEIC files use variations or metadata that browser conversion libraries may not support. Trying another file or a current browser can help.
