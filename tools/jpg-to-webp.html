Why convert JPG to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format designed for efficient compression. Depending on the image and quality setting, WebP can reduce file size while preserving good visual quality.

Adjustable WebP quality

Lower quality settings can produce smaller files, while higher settings preserve more visual detail. Compare the original and output file sizes after conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert JPG to WebP?

Choose a JPG image, set the quality, select Convert to WebP and download the result.

Will WebP always be smaller than JPG?

No. Output size depends on the source image, dimensions and chosen quality setting.

Does the converter change image dimensions?

No. This converter keeps the original width and height.

Is the JPG uploaded?

No. The conversion runs locally in your browser.
