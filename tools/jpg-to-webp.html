How to convert JPG or JPEG to WebP

Choose a JPG image, adjust the WebP quality, select Convert to WebP, then review the output size and download your file. The converter keeps the source image's width and height.

Why use WebP for web images?

WebP is designed for efficient image compression and is supported by modern browsers. Depending on the source and quality setting, it can produce a smaller download while retaining useful visual detail—helpful when preparing images for a website.

Balance WebP quality and file size

Lower quality settings can create smaller files, while higher settings preserve more detail. Because WebP is not guaranteed to beat every JPG, compare the original and output sizes shown after conversion. If dimensions also need changing, use the Image Resizer.

Local, private image conversion

Conversion runs in your browser, so the JPG is not uploaded to Utivaro. Need the reverse format change? Use WebP to JPG. For an iPhone photo, start with HEIC to JPG.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert JPG to WebP?

Choose a JPG image, set the quality, select Convert to WebP and download the result.

Will WebP always be smaller than JPG?

No. Output size depends on the source image, dimensions and chosen quality setting.

Does the converter change image dimensions?

No. This converter keeps the original width and height.

Is the JPG uploaded?

No. The conversion runs locally in your browser.
