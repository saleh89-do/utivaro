HEX, RGB and HSL

HEX represents red, green and blue channels with hexadecimal values. RGB uses numeric channel values from 0 to 255. HSL describes hue, saturation and lightness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert HEX to RGB?

Yes. Enter a valid HEX color and the RGB and HSL values update automatically.

Can I pick a color from an image?

Yes. Upload a supported image and tap or click a pixel to sample its color.

What image formats can I use?

The image picker supports common browser-readable formats such as JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is read and sampled locally in your browser.
