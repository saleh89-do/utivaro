Counting Days Between Dates: Inclusive, Exclusive and Deadlines

By Utivaro Team · Last updated September 30, 2026

The number of days between two dates depends on whether you count both ends. October 1 to October 31 is 30 days apart but 31 days if both days count. Countdowns use the first number; stays, courses and leave periods often use the second.

The question behind every date count

"How many days from October 1 to October 31?" has two correct answers: 30 or 31. The first counts the days between the dates, the way a calendar difference or elapsed time works. The second counts both the first and last day, the way you'd count days of a hotel stay, a festival or a work assignment. Most mistakes with deadlines come from mixing the two.

The Days Between Dates calculator gives the difference: the number of days you'd move forward on a calendar to get from the start date to the end date. It also shows weeks, approximate months and hours.

Which one to use

  • Countdowns and elapsed time ("how many days until my trip?"): use the difference. On the day before, the answer is 1.
  • Durations where both ends are working or active days (a project running Monday to Friday, a 7-day course, a leave request): use inclusive counting. Monday to Friday is 4 days apart but 5 days of work.
  • Hotel and rental stays: count nights, which equals the difference. Check-in October 10, check-out October 13 is 3 nights.
  • Age and anniversaries: use the difference. See How to Calculate Your Exact Age.

Deadlines: "within 30 days"

If a notice dated September 30, 2026 says you must respond within 30 days, adding 30 to the date gives Friday, October 30, 2026. Within 90 days gives Tuesday, December 29, 2026.

But legal and official deadlines often have their own rules:

  • Whether the day of the notice counts as day 0 or day 1.
  • Whether a deadline that lands on a weekend or public holiday moves to the next working day.
  • Whether "30 days" means calendar days or business days.

When the stakes are high, read the rule in the document itself, and don't wait for the last day.

Business days

The calculator counts calendar days, including weekends and holidays; it isn't a working-days calculator. To estimate business days, count full weeks as 5 days each and check the remaining days by hand. October 2026 has 31 days and 22 weekdays. Then subtract any public holidays that apply to you, which vary by country and region.

Leap years

A year has 366 days if it's divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400. So 2028 is a leap year, 2100 won't be, and 2000 was. That's why September 30, 2026 to September 30, 2027 is 365 days, but September 30, 2027 to September 30, 2028 is 366: the second span includes February 29, 2028.

Why the hours can surprise you

The calculator's hours are days × 24. In places that use daylight saving time, the real clock time across a clock change is one hour more or less. For example, a period that includes the last Sunday of October in the EU, October 25, 2026, has one extra hour. For flights, shifts and billing by the hour, count actual clock times rather than days.

Weeks and months

Weeks are days ÷ 7, so 86 days is 12.29 weeks, or 12 weeks and 2 days. Months are shown as approximate because months differ in length: 28 days is a whole month from February 1 to March 1, but not from March 1 to March 29. When a contract says "3 months", it normally means the same date three months later, not a fixed number of days.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include the end date?

No. It returns the difference between the dates. Add 1 if you want both the start and end dates counted.

What if I enter the dates the wrong way round?

The calculator still gives the number of days and indicates that the end date is before the start date.

How many days are there in a year?

365 in a common year and 366 in a leap year. Over a long period the average is 365.2425 days.

How do I count only weekdays?

Count full weeks as 5 weekdays each, add the weekdays in the remaining partial week, then subtract public holidays.
