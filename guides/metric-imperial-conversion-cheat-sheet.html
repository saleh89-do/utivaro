Metric vs Imperial: Conversion Cheat Sheet for Cooking and Travel

By Utivaro Team · Last updated September 30, 2026

An inch is exactly 2.54 cm, a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg, and °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. The tables below cover oven temperatures, US vs metric cups, travel distances and speeds, and why a 500 GB drive shows as 465 GB.

The conversions worth memorizing

A handful of factors cover most everyday situations. Several of these are exact by definition: an inch is exactly 2.54 cm and a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg.

Temperature

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32, and °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. A quick estimate from Celsius: double it and add 30. It's close for weather (20 °C → 70 °F, real value 68 °F) but too rough for ovens.

Oven temperatures

Recipes round oven settings, so a US recipe at 350 °F and a European one at 180 °C mean the same thing.

If a metric recipe says "fan" or "convection", it's usually 20 °C lower than a conventional oven setting.

Cooking: cups aren't the same everywhere

A US cup is 236.6 ml, but nutrition labels in the US round it to 240 ml, and Australia, Canada and many recipe sites use a 250 ml "metric cup". The difference is small for liquids and more noticeable in baking. Pints differ too: a US pint is 473 ml and a UK pint 568 ml, about 20% more.

For dry ingredients, cups measure volume, not weight, so a cup of flour and a cup of sugar weigh very different amounts. Approximate weights for one US cup:

Ingredient (1 US cup)Approximate weight
All-purpose flour, spooned and leveled120–130 g
Granulated sugar200 g
Butter227 g (2 US sticks)
Water or milkabout 240 g

Flour varies most, depending on how it's packed. For reliable baking, weigh ingredients when a recipe gives grams.

Fuel is sold by the liter in most countries and by the US gallon in the US: 1 US gallon is 3.785 liters.

Digital storage: why a 500 GB drive shows 465 GB

Drive makers use decimal units: 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes. Windows reports sizes in binary units, where 1 "GB" is actually 1,073,741,824 bytes (properly called a gibibyte, GiB). The same 500,000,000,000 bytes therefore appear as about 465.7 GB. No space is missing; the two numbers use different units.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilograms to pounds quickly?

Multiply by 2.2. For example, 70 kg × 2.2 = 154 lb. The exact factor is 2.20462.

Is a UK pint the same as a US pint?

No. A UK (imperial) pint is 568 ml; a US pint is 473 ml.

How many grams are in a cup?

It depends on the ingredient: about 120–130 g for flour, 200 g for sugar and 240 g for water in a US cup.

Why does my recipe turn out differently after converting?

Cup sizes differ between countries, and dry ingredients vary in how they're packed. Weighing ingredients in grams gives the most consistent results.
